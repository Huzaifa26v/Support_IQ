SupportIQ Customer Support Knowledge Base

Account Access:
Customers who cannot access their accounts should first verify their registered email address.
If they forget their password, they should use the password reset option.

Billing Support:
Customers can request clarification about their invoices and payment transactions.
Billing-related problems should be assigned to the billing support category.

Technical Support:
Customers experiencing application errors should provide the error message and describe the steps that caused the issue.
Technical issues should be reviewed by the technical support team.

Support Hours:
SupportIQ customer support operates Monday through Friday, from 9 AM to 5 PM.
